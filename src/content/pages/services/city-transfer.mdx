## What is a city transfer?

A city transfer is a pre-booked private journey between two fixed points inside Singapore, priced as a flat fare for the vehicle. It is the simplest thing this fleet does and the one most often booked at short notice: a hotel to a client office, a conference venue to a restaurant, one hotel to another on a change of stay.

Travellers call it different things. Hotel transfer usually means a leg starting or ending at a hotel. Point-to-point transfer is the operator's term and the one used in most corporate rate cards. City transfer covers everything within the island. All three describe the same booking and the same fare basis.

The alternative most people compare it against is a taxi or a ride-hailing app, and for a single unhurried trip in the middle of the day, that comparison is fair. A pre-booked transfer earns its place at the times when the apps get expensive or slow: the evening peak, rain, a Friday night in Clarke Quay, a 05:30 departure, or any moment when being sure matters more than being quick to book.

## Who books city transfers

Hotel guests with an early departure. A 05:30 pickup for a 08:00 flight is exactly when apps are least reliable and hotel taxi queues are empty. The car is booked the night before and is at the porch when you come down.

Business visitors between meetings. Two meetings on opposite sides of the CBD with an hour between them is either two bookings or one hourly block, and the driver knows which entrance takes a drop-off at each tower.

Restaurant and evening bookings. Friday evening in Clarke Quay, Boat Quay or Dempsey is peak surge time. A pre-booked return at a fixed fare removes the end-of-evening negotiation.

Families moving between hotels or to Sentosa. Luggage, children and island entry charges all sit inside one fixed fare, and the child seats are fitted before the car arrives.

Property viewings and relocation appointments. Several addresses in one afternoon across Bukit Timah, Holland Village and River Valley, usually better as an hourly booking than as separate legs.

Medical and clinic appointments. Fixed timing, a driver who waits, and a vehicle that can take a wheelchair with notice.

## Pickup points that cause trouble

Orchard Road hotels. Kerbside stopping is restricted along most of Orchard, so the pickup happens at the hotel porch. During check-in hours the porch queues, and the driver may be circling. Come down at the booked time rather than five minutes after.

Raffles Place and Marina Bay Financial Centre. Office towers have designated drop-off bays, often on a side street rather than the address you were given. Name the tower and the lobby.

Marina Bay Sands. Separate drop-offs for the hotel towers, the shops and the Sands Expo. During events, access changes.

Clarke Quay, Boat Quay and Robertson Quay. Pedestrianised in parts, with limited vehicle access in the evening. Pickups happen at the nearest road access point, agreed at booking.

Sentosa. Vehicles pay an island entry charge, which is inside the fare, and resort drop-offs are controlled during busy periods.

Residential addresses in Bukit Timah and Holland Village. Landed housing and condominium guardhouses both need the unit or block number, or the driver waits at the gate while you wait at the door.

## Fixed-fare transfer, taxi or ride-hailing?

| Price | Fixed at booking | Meter plus ERP and surcharges | Quoted per request, surges |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Booked in advance | Yes, days ahead | Limited advance booking | Advance booking with a premium |
| 05:30 pickup | Confirmed the night before | Availability thin | Availability thin |
| Rain or Friday evening | Same fare | Longer wait | Surge |
| Vehicle known | Class chosen at booking | Whatever is next | Whatever accepts |
| Child seats | Fitted before arrival | Rare | Occasional |
| Corporate invoicing | Monthly, coded | Paper receipts | Business profile receipts |
| Cost, midday single trip | Highest | Middle | Lowest |

Where a fixed fare loses: a short midday hop with no luggage and no deadline. Grab or a taxi will be cheaper and the difference is not worth defending. Book ahead when the time is early, the weather is bad, the group is large, or the meeting is one you cannot be late for.
