## What is a Seletar Airport transfer?

A Seletar transfer is ground transport arranged for a movement through Singapore's general aviation airport, coordinated with whoever is handling the aircraft. The booking is made against a tail number or a slot time rather than a commercial flight number, and it is confirmed with the handler so the driver can be positioned before the aircraft is released.

The vocabulary here comes from aviation rather than travel. Crews and flight departments talk about ground transport, crew transport or a car at the FBO; the passenger's assistant is more likely to ask for a Seletar airport chauffeur. All of it describes the same thing. What it is not is a Changi transfer with a different address, because the failure modes are different: at Seletar the risk is a slot moving by 90 minutes at short notice, not a passenger stuck in immigration.

Seletar also carries a second kind of work that Changi does not: repeat movements for the same operator. A flight department running Singapore regularly usually wants the same driver, the same vehicle class and the same handler contact each time, billed monthly. That is a standing arrangement rather than a booking.

## Who books Seletar transfers

Flight departments and aircraft operators. The booker is scheduling, not travelling, and needs the car tied to a tail number with the handler copied in. Slot changes are pushed to the driver directly rather than routed through the passenger.

Executive assistants for principals flying private. The pain is the gap between the aircraft door and the meeting: at Seletar it should be minutes. The driver is airside-adjacent at the private terminal before release, with the plate and the driver's mobile already sent.

Crews on layover. Crew movements are Business Van and Minibus work on a repeating schedule, hotel to airport and back, usually the same two or three hotels. These run as a standing block rather than individual bookings.

Charter brokers arranging the whole trip. Brokers want one supplier who can also cover the Changi leg, the cruise terminal and the hourly work, invoiced together.

## What to know before a Seletar movement

How is the pickup timed?
From the estimated wheels-down time, adjusted as the slot moves. The driver aims to be at the private terminal before the aircraft lands, and holds until the handler releases the passengers.

What happens if the slot slips?
Nothing, if we know. Send the revised ETA and the car repositions. An unnotified slip of more than an hour is charged as standby.

Which terminal building do we meet at?
The private terminal used by your handling agent. Name the handler at booking; different operators use different facilities and gates on the same field.

Is Seletar open around the clock?
Operating hours at Seletar are more restricted than at Changi and are set by the airport rather than by us. Confirm the slot with your handler; the car works to whatever the slot allows.

Can the same car do Seletar and then Changi?
Yes, and connecting a private arrival to a commercial departure is a common booking. Allow 30 to 40 minutes between the two airports outside the peak.

Do you handle luggage and freight?
Passenger luggage, yes, sized at booking. Cargo, aircraft parts or oversize items need a Business Van or Minibus and advance notice.
