Changi Airport to Singapore Cruise Centre at HarbourFront is 26 km and 28 to 40 minutes, running the East Coast Parkway then the Ayer Rajah Expressway along the southern edge of the island. HarbourFront handles regional cruises and ferries to Batam and Bintan, beside VivoCity and the Sentosa Express.

Fares start at S$90, fixed, and timing is set by your check-in cut-off, which on ferries is tighter than on cruise ships.

## How long does it take from Changi to HarbourFront?

Twenty-eight to forty minutes. The route follows the coast and skirts the southern edge of the CBD, exposing it to delays on the AYE.

## Ferry check-in and cruise boarding at HarbourFront

Ferries to Batam, Bintan and Karimun usually close check-in 30 to 45 minutes before departure and involve immigration. Aim to arrive an hour ahead and confirm the cut-off with the operator. Regional cruise boarding windows close 60 to 90 minutes before departure. For a same-day flight connection, allow at least four hours between landing and ferry departure, or five for a cruise.

## At the terminal

Singapore Cruise Centre sits within the HarbourFront complex next to VivoCity, an MRT interchange and the Sentosa Express. The driver takes the terminal drop-off rather than the mall entrance, which is easy to confuse on foot with luggage.

This is not Marina Bay Cruise Centre. If your documents name Marina Bay, that is a different terminal about 8 km east.
