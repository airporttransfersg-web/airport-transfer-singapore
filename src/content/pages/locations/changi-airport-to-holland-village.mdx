Changi Airport to Holland Village is 26 km and 28 to 40 minutes, running the Pan Island Expressway west and dropping down through Farrer Road or Commonwealth. Holland Village is a residential and expatriate district rather than a hotel belt, so most arrivals here are people moving in, coming home, or staying in a serviced apartment for a few weeks.

Fares start at S$90, fixed, and the address detail matters more than on a hotel route, because condominium security will not admit a car on a name alone.

## How long does it take from Changi to Holland Village?

Twenty-eight to forty minutes across the island. Like Bukit Timah, this is a cross-island run on the PIE, so the expressway dictates the time rather than the last mile.

| Travel period | Typical drive time |
| --- | ---: |
| Weekday 06:00–07:30 | 26–32 min |
| Weekday 07:30–09:30 | 38–50 min |
| Weekday 10:00–16:30 | 28–38 min |
| Weekday 17:30–19:30 | 38–48 min |
| Evenings and weekends | 26–36 min |

Holland Village itself is quiet outside weekend evenings, when the restaurants around Lorong Mambong and One Holland Village fill up and kerbside space disappears.
