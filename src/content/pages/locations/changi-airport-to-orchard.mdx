Changi Airport to Orchard Road is 20 km and takes 20 to 30 minutes outside the peak, by the East Coast Parkway and into the city from the east. It is the most-booked route on this site, because Orchard holds the largest concentration of hotels in Singapore and almost every one of them has restricted kerbside access, which makes the drop-off point the part worth getting right.

A private transfer on this route is a fixed fare from S$90, with the driver meeting you inside your arrival terminal and dropping you at the hotel porch rather than on the road outside.

## How long does it take from Changi to Orchard Road?

Twenty to thirty minutes for most of the day. The drive runs the East Coast Parkway along the coast, then turns inland either at Nicoll Highway or via the Kallang-Paya Lebar Expressway when the ECP is heavy. The distance barely changes; the traffic does.

| Travel period | Typical drive time |
| --- | ---: |
| Weekday 06:00–07:30 | 20–25 min |
| Weekday 07:30–09:30 | 30–40 min, inbound peak |
| Weekday 10:00–16:30 | 20–28 min |
| Weekday 17:30–19:30 | 30–40 min |
| Evenings and weekends | 20–30 min |
| Wet weather | Add 10 minutes at any hour |

The last two kilometres are the variable part. Orchard Road itself moves slowly on Saturday afternoons and during the December shopping season, and hotel porches queue during the 14:00 to 16:00 check-in window.

Fixed at booking. Includes the Changi airport entry fee, ERP gantries on the way in, parking, fuel and GST. The fare does not change if your flight lands late, and a return leg to Changi booked at the same time goes on the same confirmation.

Late-night pickups between 23:00 and 06:59 carry a loading of S$20.

## Where the driver drops you on Orchard Road

Orchard's hotels sit along a three-kilometre stretch with different access arrangements, and kerbside stopping is restricted along most of the main road.

<details class="disclosure border-b border-mist"><summary>Upper Orchard and Tanglin</summary><div>Shangri-La, Goodwood Park and the hotels around Tanglin Road. Access is from the side roads rather than Orchard Boulevard, and the porches are set back, so the drop is at the door.</div></details>

<details class="disclosure border-b border-mist"><summary>Central Orchard</summary><div>The hotels attached to ION, Ngee Ann City and Wisma Atria share a congested stretch and their porches are entered from Orchard Turn or Paterson Road. Give the hotel name and the driver takes the correct approach.</div></details>

<details class="disclosure border-b border-mist"><summary>Somerset and lower Orchard</summary><div>Hotels near Somerset and Dhoby Ghaut are entered from Killiney Road, Devonshire or Somerset Road. During events at the Istana or a road closure on Orchard, access shifts to the parallel roads.</div></details>

<details class="disclosure border-b border-mist"><summary>Orchard serviced apartments and residences</summary><div>These use guardhouse access, so the block or unit number matters more than the street address.</div></details>

If your accommodation is a residence rather than a hotel, give the block number at booking. Condominium security will not let a car past the gate on a name alone.

## Which vehicle for this route?

Orchard trips are usually short on distance and long on luggage, because most passengers are arriving for a stay rather than a meeting.

| Vehicle | Seats and luggage | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Premium MPV | 2 travellers with 2 large cases | The default on this route |
| Executive MPV | 3 to 4 travellers, or 2 travellers with more than 3 large cases | The most common upgrade |
| Business Van | 5 to 6 travellers with a full luggage load | Long-haul family arrivals |
| Minibus | 7 to 8 travellers | Groups arriving together for one hotel |
| 40-seater coach | Larger groups | Conference and tour arrivals |

Coming back to Changi at the end of a Singapore stay, count your bags again. Orchard is where the extra suitcase gets bought.

## Who takes this route

### Leisure travellers checking into an Orchard hotel

The arrival is the first hour of a holiday and the queue at the pickup bay is the worst possible start to it. Pre-booking replaces that with a name board and a car that is already parked.

### Business travellers with a same-day meeting

Orchard is 10 to 15 minutes from Raffles Place outside the peak, so a morning arrival can drop bags and be in the CBD before lunch. If the day continues, an hourly booking from the airport works out cheaper than three legs.

### Families on a long-haul arrival

Two adults, two children, four cases and a pram is a Business Van, and the child seats are fitted before the vehicle leaves.

## Private car, taxi or MRT from Changi to Orchard?

| Option | Typical fare | Journey time | Pickup | Drop-off |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Private transfer | S$90 fixed | 20–30 min | Driver waiting inside arrivals | Hotel porch |
| Taxi | Metered plus airport surcharge | 20–30 min | Rank queue, longer at peak | Kerbside |
| Grab or Gojek | Quoted on request, can surge | 20–30 min plus 5–25 min wait | Pickup bay | Kerbside |
| MRT | A few dollars | About 45 minutes with a change at Tanah Merah, plus the walk from Orchard station | Walk to the station at T2 or T3 | Station, then walk |

The MRT is genuinely good on this route if you are travelling light: take the East West Line to City Hall, then the North South Line to Orchard. With two large cases and a child, it is not.

## Orchard Road to Changi Airport: the return leg

Work back from the check-in cut-off. Long-haul check-in generally closes 60 minutes before departure, and Changi asks passengers to be at the terminal about three hours ahead for long-haul flights.

| Flight time | Suggested Orchard departure |
| --- | --- |
| 08:00 | Leave Orchard about 05:15 |
| 12:00 | Leave about 09:15, allowing for the tail of the morning peak |
| 18:00 | Leave about 14:45, before the evening peak builds |
| 23:55 | Leave about 20:45 |

Add 15 minutes if you are leaving between 17:30 and 19:30 on a weekday. The driver waits at the porch for 15 minutes from the booked time at no charge.
