Changi Airport to Sentosa is 27 km and 30 to 45 minutes, along the East Coast Parkway and the Ayer Rajah Expressway to Telok Blangah, then over the Sentosa Gateway onto the island. Vehicles pay an island entry charge, which is inside the quoted fare, and drop-off points on the island are controlled during busy periods.

Fares start at S$90, fixed. Sentosa is the one Changi destination where the last two kilometres are on a private island with its own traffic rules, and where the difference between hotels is more than a street name.

## How long does it take from Changi to Sentosa?

Thirty to forty-five minutes, of which the last five to ten are on the island itself.

| Travel period | Typical drive time |
| --- | ---: |
| Weekday 06:00–07:30 | 28–35 min |
| Weekday 07:30–09:30 | 40–55 min |
| Weekday 10:00–16:30 | 30–40 min |
| Weekday 17:30–19:30 | 40–50 min |
| Weekend afternoons | 35–55 min |

On a Saturday afternoon or public holiday, the queue at the gateway can add 15 minutes on its own, and events at Resorts World add more.

## Drop-off points on Sentosa

<details class="disclosure border-b border-mist"><summary>Beach-front resorts</summary><div>Individual porches along Siloso and Tanjong have direct vehicle access, straightforward on arrival but slower to leave on weekend afternoons.</div></details>
<details class="disclosure border-b border-mist"><summary>Sentosa Cove</summary><div>Residences and the marina sit at the far eastern end, about 10 minutes past the resorts. Most addresses need a block or house number for the guardhouse.</div></details>
<details class="disclosure border-b border-mist"><summary>Hilltop hotels and venues</summary><div>Capella, Sofitel, Sentosa Golf Club and event spaces have their own access arrangements, some restricted during tournaments.</div></details>

The island's roads are a one-way loop in places, so a wrong entrance is not a short correction. The hotel name at booking prevents that.

## Private car, taxi or public transport to Sentosa?

| Option | Practical difference |
| --- | --- |
| Private transfer | S$90 fixed, island entry included; resort porch or residence gate |
| Taxi | Metered plus airport and island entry charges |

The Sentosa Express is pleasant for a day trip from a city hotel and poor with a family and five suitcases. That is a different journey from a pre-booked airport transfer.
