Changi Airport to Clarke Quay is 21 km and 22 to 35 minutes by the East Coast Parkway and Nicoll Highway. The drive is straightforward. The arrival is not, because Clarke Quay is partly pedestrianised and its riverside frontage has no vehicle access at all, so every drop-off happens at a road behind the quay rather than at the water.

Fares start at S$90, fixed, and the driver takes you to the correct road access point for your hotel rather than to the nearest recognisable landmark.

## How long does it take from Changi to Clarke Quay?

Twenty-two to thirty-five minutes for most of the day. On a Friday night the roads around the quay are busy with drop-offs and the driver may stop at the nearest legal point rather than directly outside a hotel entrance.

Because some Clarke Quay drops involve walking the last 100 metres, luggage weight matters more here than on a hotel-porch route like Orchard.

Clarke Quay station sits at the edge of the district, so the MRT works well with light luggage. After midnight it does not run, which is when many bookings on this route happen.
