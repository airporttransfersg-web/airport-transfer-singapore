Changi Airport to Bukit Timah is about 28 km across the island by the Pan Island Expressway, 30 to 45 minutes depending on the hour. It is the longest of the common city-bound Changi routes and the one with the most varied destinations: landed houses, condominiums with guardhouses, serviced apartments and international and local schools along Bukit Timah Road and Dunearn Road.

Fares start at S$90, fixed, and most of this work is residential rather than hotel, which changes what the driver needs to know before setting off.

| Detail | What to expect |
| --- | --- |

| Drive time | 30–45 minutes, up to 55 in the peak |
| Route | Pan Island Expressway, exiting at Adam Road or Clementi Road |

## How long does it take from Changi to Bukit Timah?

Thirty to forty-five minutes across the island on the PIE. Unlike the city routes, this drive crosses the whole width of Singapore, so the variable is the expressway rather than the last mile.

| Travel period | Typical drive time |
| --- | ---: |
| Weekday 06:00–07:30 | 28–35 min |
| Weekday 07:30–09:30 | 40–55 min |
| Weekday 10:00–16:30 | 30–40 min |
| Weekday 17:30–19:30 | 40–50 min |
| Evenings and weekends | 28–40 min |

School traffic along Bukit Timah Road and Dunearn Road adds several minutes around 07:00–08:00 and again around 14:00 on weekdays.

## Private car, taxi or MRT to Bukit Timah?

| Option | Typical fare | Journey time | Practical detail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Private transfer | S$90 fixed | 30–45 min | Door, past the guardhouse; luggage sized at booking |
| Taxi | Metered + airport surcharge | 30–45 min | Kerb or gate; sedan boot |
| MRT | Cheapest | About 70 min | Downtown Line, then a walk or short ride |

The MRT reaches Bukit Timah on the Downtown Line, but stations sit along the main road and most addresses are a walk or short ride away. With luggage, this is the weakest MRT route of the common Changi destinations.
