## Information to prepare before requesting a quote

Start with the flight number and travel date, then add the exact pickup and destination addresses. Include passenger count, the number and approximate size of bags, any child-seat request, and accessibility or mobility requirements.

For a departure, share the desired airport arrival time or flight departure time. For an arrival, include the name that should appear on any meeting sign, if that service is confirmed.

## Choosing a vehicle for passengers and luggage

A vehicle's seat count is not the same as its practical capacity once luggage is included. A Business Sedan may suit up to 3 passengers, a Premium MPV up to 6, and an Executive Van up to 7, but the exact fit depends on the bags and equipment travelling with the group.

| Category | Working passenger guide | Best considered for |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Business Sedan | Up to 3 | Solo travellers, couples and small business parties |
| Premium MPV | Up to 6 | Families and groups needing flexible luggage space |
| Executive Van | Up to 7 | Larger groups, several cases or hosted delegations |

Specific model or equivalent, luggage fit and current rate should be confirmed in the quotation.

## Planning an airport arrival transfer

Confirm the terminal meeting instructions before travel. Airport meeting point, included waiting time, flight-monitoring process and charges after the grace period — VERIFY with the operator.

If travellers will not have mobile data on arrival, agree a backup contact method in advance.

## Planning an airport departure transfer

Allow for travel time, the airline's check-in recommendation and possible peak-period road conditions. Share any intermediate pickup or stop when requesting the quote, because it changes the route and collection time.

## Child seats and accessibility requirements

State the child's age, approximate weight and the seat type required. Availability, suitability and charge — VERIFY. For wheelchair or mobility support, explain whether the passenger can transfer into a standard seat and provide equipment dimensions where relevant.

## Questions to confirm before paying

1. What exact vehicle category or equivalent is being quoted?
2. Are airport, parking and late-night charges included?
3. What waiting time and cancellation rules apply?
4. Where will the traveller meet the chauffeur?
5. Who should be contacted if the flight or itinerary changes?

## Airport transfer booking checklist

- Flight number and travel date
- Pickup and destination
- Passenger and luggage details
- Child-seat or accessibility needs
- Meeting point and contact number
- Total price, inclusions and policy confirmation
