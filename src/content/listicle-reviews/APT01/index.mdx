*What each option looks like in the 30 minutes after you clear customs, and the 10 pre-booked operators worth comparing.*


## Short answer

For families and groups with checked bags, a pre-booked private car is the best way out of Changi: a driver waits at the arrival-hall exit with your name, the fare is agreed before you land, and the vehicle is sized to your luggage. MySingaporeDriver covers the widest set of arrival points, including the VIP Complex, CIP terminal and Seletar. Airport Transfer Singapore publishes every fare by bag count. A taxi suits one or two people travelling light; ride-hailing apps work off-peak but give no luggage guarantee.

Quick picks:

- Arrivals at any terminal, including private jets: MySingaporeDriver.
- Family of four with four large cases: Airport Transfer Singapore's Executive MPV (4 passengers, 4 large cases, S$105).
- Business traveller in a sedan: Singapore Chauffeur Service (S$105 airport run).
- Two people, carry-on only, mid-afternoon: the taxi queue.
- Late arrival with no booking: the Ground Transport Concierge counter, open 24 hours.

## What happens after you clear customs

1. You collect bags from the belt and walk through customs into the public arrival hall.
2. With a pre-booked car, a driver stands at the exit doors holding your name. You walk to the car together.
3. With a taxi, you follow signs to the taxi stand and join the queue.
4. With an app, you book once you have signal, then walk to the ride-hailing pick-up point for your terminal.
5. With the counter car, you go to the Ground Transport Concierge desk and book on the spot.

The difference shows up from step 2 onward: how long you stand around, whether the car fits your bags, and whether the price can move.

## Is it better to use Grab or pre-book a private transfer from Changi?

Pre-book if you have more than two large bags, children who need seats, a group above six, or a late-night landing. Use Grab or another app if you are travelling light, landing at a quiet hour, and happy to wait for a car type that fits. The app price is set when you book, so it can be higher at busy times; a pre-booked fare is agreed days before. The meeting point differs too: an app driver meets you at the ride-hailing pick-up point, while a pre-booked driver comes into the arrival area with your name. Changi lists Grab, Gojek, Zig, Tada, Ryde and Geolah as the apps that pick up at Terminals 1 to 4.

## Which option to book

- If you have three or more large cases, book a private car and choose the vehicle by bag count.
- If you are travelling with children who need seats, book at least 48 hours ahead and request the seats by age and weight through our [booking form](/contact/).
- If your group is above six, book a minibus or coach, not several taxis.
- If you are arriving by private jet, book an operator that lists the VIP Complex, CIP terminal or Seletar.
- If you are one or two people with carry-on bags and land before 5 pm, the taxi queue is usually fine.
- If you land after midnight with no booking, go to the Ground Transport Concierge counter for a fixed fare.
