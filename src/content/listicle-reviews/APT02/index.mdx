*Car seats, pushchairs and a case per person: which operators can take all of it out of Changi in one vehicle, and what each one charges.*


## Short answer

A family landing at Changi with children and checked bags needs three things settled before the flight: a vehicle sized to the cases, not the headcount; child seats fitted before the driver sets off; and a quote that separates the fare, free waiting and any delay charges. Airport Transfer Singapore publishes the case count for every vehicle, from three cases in a Premium MPV to eight in a minibus. Limousine Service Singapore prices each extra in advance. MySingaporeDriver names infant capsules, toddler seats and boosters, with vehicle restrictions for capsules.

Quick picks:

- Four passengers and four cases: the Executive MPV from Airport Transfer Singapore, S$105 on arrival. Confirm the child-seat layout and any pushchair space before booking.
- A family landing after 11 pm who wants every extra priced first: Limousine Service Singapore.
- A baby under 12 months who needs a rear-facing capsule: MySingaporeDriver.
- Eight relatives and eight cases on one fare: Airport Transfer Singapore's minibus (S$130 arrival).
- More than 13 people: Limo Maxi Cabs' 23-seat Mitsubishi Rosa, or a coach.

## What happens after you clear customs

1. Checked strollers usually come out on the oversized-baggage belt, which can add time. Ask the airline where any checked child seat will be returned, and collect everything before walking out.
2. With a confirmed meet-and-greet, follow the agreed arrival-hall meeting instructions. At Airport Transfer Singapore that's the glass exit doors nearest your baggage belt; follow the meeting instructions for your terminal.
3. The child seats should already be in the car. Check the seat type against your child's age and weight before anyone climbs in.
4. Confirm space for cases and the pushchair, as well as fitted child seats, before setting off.
5. Before booking, confirm that the vehicle can carry every passenger, child seat, case and pushchair together.

## Which option to book

- If you have more cases than people, size the car by the case count: Airport Transfer Singapore lists it for every class.
- If you're landing after 11 pm and want the total fixed first, Limousine Service Singapore publishes the late charge and the seat price.
- If your baby needs a rear-facing capsule, ask MySingaporeDriver which vehicle carries it, and book that one.
- If plans may move, Singapore Chauffeur Service gives one free amendment and free cancellation up to 48 hours out.
- If the family wants the car to wait at Jewel or the hotel, book Hourly Chauffeur Singapore by the hour.
- If you want the pickup door fixed before you fly, Black Car Service Singapore and Corporate Car Service Singapore confirm it in writing.
- If you're more than 13 people, book a 23-seat bus or a coach, not three cars. Request it through our [city and hotel transfer page](/services/city-transfer/).
