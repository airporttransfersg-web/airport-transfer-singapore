### Terms (both sites)

- Booking by enquiry form or email only; no phone line. BCS replies within 1 to 2 working days; CCS within one day. An automatic acknowledgement is not a booking; confirmation only on written acceptance of the quotation.
- Cancellation, amendment, late-night, extra-stop and overtime charges are set per quotation; no fixed schedule is published.
- Peak dates (F1 race weekend, Singapore Airshow, New Year's Eve) are quoted separately.
- BCS: payment by the passenger; company invoicing only if individually agreed. CCS: corporate invoicing available, terms agreed in writing; PO number or cost centre can go on the invoice; no automatic credit accounts.
- Legal entity: neither site names one. BCS address 1B Cantonment Road, #44-19; CCS address Paya Lebar Square, #06-28.
