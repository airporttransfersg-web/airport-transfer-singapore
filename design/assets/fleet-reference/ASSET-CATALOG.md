## Production preparation

- Confirm the operator owns or has permission to publish every photograph.
- Registration plates have been removed from the derivatives in `../fleet-public-safe/`; use only those files in the website.
- Remove or obtain permission for third-party fleet branding, particularly the `RUANG` and `KEEPER` marks.
- Check incidental people, nearby vehicles, windshield permits, hotel signage, and other identifying details before publication.
- Apply one restrained, natural colour grade across the set; do not alter vehicle models or generate misleading vehicle details.
- Keep source files unchanged. Create separate web derivatives in AVIF/WebP and retain JPEG fallbacks.
